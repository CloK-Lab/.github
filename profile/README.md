# $\overline{\text{𝕂}}$ — Understand and Guide Mathematical Research

CloK is a learning and research community. We bring the structure of mathematical knowledge into view, helping people understand what is known and find promising directions for discovery.

Mathematics grows when ideas connect. We want to make those connections easier to see across individual arguments, entire libraries, and different ways of expressing mathematics.

Our starting point is **formalized mathematics**, where reasoning can be checked and its foundations traced. We bring mathematical insight and computational tools together to help researchers see what they can build on, how ideas fit together, and which questions are worth pursuing.

## Learn and build with us

We learn through shared reading, formalizing mathematics, and building tools for exploring mathematical knowledge. Researchers, students, and tool builders are welcome to join us.
