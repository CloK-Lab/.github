# $\overline{\text{𝕂}}$ — Closure of Knowledge

## Study Verifiable Knowledge at Scale

CloK is a research and learning community connecting theoretical researchers and industry practitioners. We study **AI safety, formal verification, and the games and incentives behind collaboration**.

We want practical problems to inform theoretical research, and methods and insights from theory to be tested in real settings.

### What we study

- **AI safety and industrial practice** — turning safety requirements into precise specifications, verifiable properties, and practical safeguards, while understanding the assumptions and boundaries of each guarantee.
- **Mathematics, formalization, and verification** — exploring Lean, AI for mathematics, and the connections between mathematical ideas, formal proofs, and software and industrial systems.
- **Games, incentives, and cooperation** — studying how people, AI, and organizations share information, distribute the costs of verification, and cooperate when their goals differ.
- **Open science and DeSci** — exploring funding mechanisms, cryptographic tools, and knowledge markets that support research, preserve provenance, and recognize contributions, alongside their risks and costs.

### Learn and build with us

We learn through shared reading, discussion of practical problems, formalizing arguments, and building small tools. Whether you bring a theoretical question, a technical method, or an unresolved problem from production, you are welcome to study it with us.
